## 1\. Purpose

This policy tells staff how to use company devices, accounts, and information safely, so that patient and client information stays protected.

## 2\. Scope

All 50 employees, plus contractors and vendor staff who use company devices, accounts, or information, in the Newark office or anywhere else they work. This policy and the related training must be available in English and Thai.

## 3\. Roles and responsibilities

|Role|Responsibility|
|-|-|
|All staff|Follow this policy, complete training, and report problems right away|
|Managers|Make sure their team knows this policy and follows it|
|Payroll and Finance Staff|Follow the extra rule for payment and bank-detail changes, and complete the extra training|
|IT Manager|Keep company devices updated, support staff who report problems, and review reports of suspicious messages|
|HR Manager|Track who has completed training|
|Compliance and Privacy Officer|Own this policy, arrange training in both languages, and review reports|

## 4\. Policy statements

|#|Statement|Control it supports|Risk it reduces|
|-|-|-|-|
|AU-1|Staff must use company devices and accounts for company work. Staff must not store or send company information through personal email, text messages, or personal cloud storage.|PR.DS-01|R-04|
|AU-2|Staff must keep passwords and MFA codes private. They must not share them with anyone, including coworkers, managers, or IT.|PR.AA-01|R-01, R-06|
|AU-3|Staff must report a suspicious email, text, or call to IT right away. They must not click its links or open its attachments.|PR.AT-02|R-01|
|AU-4|Before changing anyone's bank or payment details, staff must confirm the request by calling the person on a phone number already on file. An email confirmation alone is not enough.|PR.AT-02|R-12|
|AU-5|Patient information (PHI) may be kept only in approved systems: the billing platform, the clearinghouse portal, company Microsoft 365, and the office file server. Staff must not copy it to personal devices, USB drives, or personal accounts.|PR.DS-01|R-04|
|AU-6|Staff must lock their screen when they step away, and must not leave a company laptop unattended in a vehicle or public place.|PR.DS-01|R-04|
|AU-7|Staff must report a lost or stolen device to IT right away, and no later than one hour after noticing.|PR.DS-01|R-04|
|AU-8|Staff working outside the office must reach company systems only through the VPN, signed in with their own account and MFA code. They must not use public Wi-Fi to reach patient information without the VPN.|PR.IR-01|R-05|
|AU-9|Staff must keep printed documents that contain PHI in a locked cabinet when not in use, and must put them in the shredding bin when done. They must never put them in the recycling.|PR.AA-06|R-13|
|AU-10|Staff must install security updates on their devices within seven days of IT asking.|PR.PS-02|R-02|
|AU-11|Every employee must complete security awareness training within 30 days of hire and once every year after that. Payroll and finance staff must also complete training on payment fraud. Training must be offered in English and Thai.|PR.AT-01, PR.AT-02|R-01, R-12, R-14|
|AU-12|The company may monitor the use of its systems and devices to keep them secure. Staff should not expect privacy on company systems.|DE.CM-03|R-09|

## 5\. Exceptions

A person who cannot follow a statement must ask in writing (email is fine) to the Compliance and Privacy Officer, explaining the reason, the risk, and the extra safeguard that will be used. The COO must approve it. Every exception must have an end date no more than 90 days away and must be recorded in the exceptions log.

## 6\. Enforcement

Not following this policy may lead to retraining, loss of access, or disciplinary action, depending on how serious it is. Anyone who reports a mistake or a problem in good faith will not be penalized.
