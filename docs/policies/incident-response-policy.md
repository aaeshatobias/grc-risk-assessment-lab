## 1\. Purpose

This policy makes sure everyone knows what to do, and who to tell, when something goes wrong with company data or systems. It aims to limit the harm, meet legal and contract duties, and restore normal work quickly.

## 2\. Scope

All 50 employees, plus contractors and vendor staff who use company systems or handle company data. It covers any event that harms, or might harm, the privacy, accuracy, or availability of that data or those systems.



## 3\. Roles and responsibilities

|Role|Responsibility|
|-|-|
|All staff|Report a suspected incident right away and follow instructions from the response team|
|Managers|Make sure their team knows how to report, and help gather facts|
|Incident Lead (Chief Operating Officer)|Decide whether a report is an incident, run the response, and keep the incident record|
|Technical Lead (IT Manager)|Contain the incident, preserve evidence, and restore systems|
|Privacy Lead (Compliance and Privacy Officer)|Decide whether patient information was involved and manage notices|
|Communications Lead (Founder and CEO)|Speak for the company to clients, insurers, and the public|

## 5\. Exceptions

During an active incident, only the Incident Lead may change a step in this policy, and must record what was changed and why. At other times, a person who cannot follow a statement must ask in writing to the Compliance and Privacy Officer, explaining the reason, the risk, and the extra safeguard. The Founder and CEO must approve it. Every exception must have an end date no more than 90 days away and must be recorded in the exceptions log.

## 6\. Enforcement

Failing to report an incident promptly may lead to retraining or disciplinary action, depending on how serious it is. Anyone who reports a problem in good faith will not be penalized.
