## 1\. Purpose

This policy makes sure only the right people can reach Anita \& Daughters systems and patient information, and only for as long as their job requires it.

## 2\. Scope

All 50 employees, plus contractors and vendor staff who use company systems or handle company data, whether they work in the Newark office or remotely. It covers every account on Microsoft 365, the VPN, the billing platform, the clearinghouse portal, the file server, the backup service, and any other company system.

## 3\. Roles and responsibilities

|Role|Responsibility|
|-|-|
|All staff|Use only their own account, keep their credentials private, and report anything unusual|
|Managers|Approve access requests for their team, tell HR and IT about role changes and departures before they happen, and complete the quarterly access review|
|HR Manager|Confirm start dates, role changes, and last days to IT|
|IT Manager|Create, change, and disable accounts as approved, protect administrator accounts, and remove access found in reviews|
|Compliance and Privacy Officer|Own this policy, track exceptions, and check that reviews are done|
|Founder and CEO|Approve this policy and any high-risk exception|

## 5\. Exceptions

A person who cannot follow a statement must ask in writing (email is fine) to the Compliance and Privacy Officer. The request must give the reason, the risk, and the extra safeguard that will be used. The COO must approve it. Every exception must have an end date no more than 90 days away, must be recorded in the exceptions log, and must be reviewed before it ends.

## 6\. Enforcement

Not following this policy may lead to retraining, loss of access, or disciplinary action, depending on how serious it is. Anyone who reports a problem in good faith will not be penalized.
