## What the company does

Anita & Daughters takes over the billing work for small practices. Staff turn each visit into a charge, submit claims to insurers through a clearinghouse, follow up on unpaid or denied claims, and answer questions from the practices and their patients. Staff support clients and patients in English and Thai, so policies and training should be available in both languages.

## Data types

- **Protected health information (PHI):** patient names, dates of birth, insurance IDs, diagnosis and procedure codes
- **Payment card data:** card numbers entered on the payment page, handled through a payment processor
- **Client contracts and pricing:** agreements with each practice
- **Employee and payroll records:** HR files, pay, and tax information
- **Company financial records:** invoices, bank activity, and accounting data

## Third parties

- **Clearinghouse:** receives claims and returns insurer responses
- **Cloud backup vendor:** stores backup copies off site
- **Payment processor:** handles patient card payments
- **Outside IT help desk:** after-hours support, with remote access to staff devices
